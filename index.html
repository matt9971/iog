关于我们About

一家从青年出发的国际组织智库A think tank that starts with young people

国组智库成立于 2025 年 1 月 26 日，由中国人民大学文化科技园孵化，是中国第一家聚焦国际组织研究与实务的新型青年智库。 Founded on 26 January 2025 and incubated by the Culture & Science Park of Renmin University of China, IOG is the first Chinese think tank devoted specifically to the study and practice of international organizations.

IOING CHINA

全球最大的中国国际组织青年人才社群The largest network of Chinese youth in international organizations

中国国际组织实习生交流社群构想于 2022 年 12 月 27 日，2023 年 2 月 4 日正式成立。截至 2025 年 6 月 10 日，成员遍布 67 个国家、超过 100 个国际组织。它的实质是国际组织领域的一张全球信息与资源网络。 The International Organization Interns Networking Group was conceived on 27 December 2022 and formally founded on 4 February 2023. As of 10 June 2025 its members are spread across 67 countries and more than 100 international organizations — in effect, a global information and resource network for the field.

战略目标What we are building

为全球各国学者、尤其是青年学者，提供一个前沿、权威、严谨的学术研究与思想交流平台；并以成为世界一流的 SSCI 英文国际学术期刊为目标，为优化和完善全球治理作出学术贡献。 A rigorous and forward-looking platform for scholars everywhere, and especially for early-career researchers — with the ambition of becoming a world-class SSCI-indexed journal and contributing scholarship to better global governance.

“青椒之声”青年学者访谈Voices of Young Scholars

每月 1–2 篇，访谈中外高校全球治理领域的青年学者，记录他们的问题意识与研究路径。 One to two interviews each month with early-career scholars of global governance in China and abroad, on the questions they are asking and how they got there.
